---
title: The Dreamers Finally Wake Up
kind: story
mark: ☼ 醒 ☼
---

After an endless Night, the sun finally arises, conquering the darkness guarding its throne. Rays of light reveal themselves from behind peaks of snow, shimmering the outlines of everything, creating the Glow. 4 years this Night has covered the Wastes. 4 years the Kuroni have suffered. From under the sun, a single rider strides forward on his mechanical steed. With blood and oil dripping behind him, he rides closer to the walls of Xurell.

At first, from within the walls, only a few cheers and the scuffle of footsteps can be heard. As more and more people wake, however, the noise grows louder as everyone rushes to call their family and friends to the outer wall.

Soon, the entire city is at its feet, cheering a single phrase:

“Long live the Sun!”

Finally, within audible range, the rider looks up at the walls and the people clambering over it. His helmet disguises any emotions. Yet, the rider seems to sit a little taller after realizing the presence of the public’s eye.

After an eternity of roars and cheers from the fortress, the rider finally reaches the gates.

Slowly, he hobbles off his mount, clearly favoring his right leg. From behind his back, he pulls out a ring, recites an incantation, and materializes a sleek metal walking cane.

He raises a handgun above his head and fires it. A tracer round speeds upwards for only a second before the rider falls limp, a small blue dart protruding out of their neck, as the crowd silences..

A second passes.

Two. Three. Four.

The walls start to lean, leaning and leaning.

And at the point of no return,

and as if the floodgates to hell had opened,

The walls fall as a roar of sound shatter flesh, skin, and bone.

The dreamers finally wake up
