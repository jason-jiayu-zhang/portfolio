---
title: a sea of stars
kind: story
mark: ✧ 星 ✧
---

You’ve been counting seconds for a long time.

71973484.

71973485.

How many months? You don’t know. If you try to figure it out you’ll lose count.

71973486.

71973487.

There’s nothing else to do except count to infinity in the umbra.

71973488.

71973489.

Even the white noise you’re used to on Earth has been muted.

*Night can be two things: peace or chaos.*

And yet you hear something.

Finally.

A voice, maybe an intercom, maybe three rooms over, begins to sound, interrupting your count. Sound. Sound? You haven’t heard sound in 70418290 seconds. Or was it 70821490? You’ve lost track. It’s not important now. What are they saying?

You lean a bit closer to the wall, pushing your weight to get your ear to the wall. Pushing, further and further, you inch yourself closer and close- What’s this? Something’s holding your right leg in place. Why can’t you move? You pull your leg, pull it harder and harder- oh, the chain. A cold, iron—is it iron?—shackle they put on you when they pushed you in here. You forgot.

That’s right, them. Them. The dark. They held you prisoner against your will—your dignity. Held you hostage against an entire city—your people. Held you as an example of what happens when anyone defies them—your enemy.

Stuck in your mind, you continue to hallucinate past events: the World’s War, the Suppression, the Rise, the Fall.

“Weapon. Tool.”

The night echoes into your ears.

“Wielded. Used.”

The silence is absolute yet deafening.

“Dull. Spent.”

The walls of the cell hold everything away.

*Night is uniform. Calm. Stagnant. With no light to alter, nothing alters. Everything is equally dark as the next.*

Something is here.

You jump in fright, scrambling across the room after realizing its presence.

Human? Humanoid. It’s not a human. Everything about it is black. Light? How can you see it? How can you see? It’s been dark this entire time. Dark longer than it has ever felt before.

Looking at each of the walls, a small flap on the far wall has opened, letting in a tiny amount of fluorescent light, enough to see yet still too bright for your eyes.

Looking back to the humanoid. Everything about it is black—no, it is black. It is the definition of darkness. Of blankness. Of nothing. Slowly, the figure begins to extend its hand out to you. Completely still, you wait for it to finish. In its hands is a key. A diamond key. An escape.

From the wall with the window, another hole of light appears. This time a keyhole.

A key, a keyhole. A key, a keyhole. Looking between the two, you’re too curious to not try.

Slowly, you reach for the key in the humanoid’s hand.

It doesn’t move.

Closer and closer you stretch.

It doesn’t move.

Almost touching it, your fingertips reach past the end of the chain.

It doesn’t move.

Grasping the key in your hand, you slowly move backward towards the door.

It doesn’t move.

Looking towards the keyhole, you take a second to turn again, checking if the figure is still in its corner.

It’s vanished.

In a panic, you shove the key in the keyhole, shove the door open, nothing happens, grab the lip of the window, push it into the wall, willing it to move, it moves, don’t question how, just run, run out of the room, far, far away, as far as you can run, further. further.. further…

Out of breath. You take a moment to rest. Out, of, breath. Out. of. brea-

What’s this sound?

Sound. You can hear again. You’ve never felt so happy to hear the white noise more in your life.

..

Wait a second.

It’s not white noise.

Second. Seconds? You stopped counting. It’s not important.

Distantly, you identify a soft tune whispering across the hall.

This song… You know this song. Or the motif at least. It’s been warped and transposed through what seems like 7 different keys all at once, a mystical chaos in your mind. Before you can fully take it in though, the tip of a halberd pokes your spine.

“走。” A voice orders from behind you.

You can’t tell what he’s saying. He sounds like he wants you to go forward.

Seeing your hesitation he pushes you again with his weapon, clearly impatient.

“別讓我再說。走。”

You seem to have no choice but to walk.

*And yet. Night is unknown. Chaotic. Stray. With no light, to see you see nothing. Everything is uncertain as the next.*

A door emerges from the dark. It has a window. A small window. After how long? You stopped counting. Seconds aren’t important anymore. What's in the window? A ray of light. Shining. Beckoning. Almost, for you to enter. That’s what’s important.

The guards pause, causing you to stumble forward with the sudden lurch of the chain.

“进去。” The guard growls, shoving you into the door.

Look up. Look at the window—a crystal window. What’s there? Your eyes level at the height of the window and the beam of light—beam of hope—within. An opaque barrier shimmers at the back of the room, tilted slightly upwards at the bottom of a semicircle of stairs. You’re startled when the door—gracefully—slides into the wall. And before that moment of surprise even begins to pass, you feel the butt of a halberd as it shoves you into the room, sending you sprawling to the floor. The door slams shut behind you

Over an intercom system, the guard speaks again. “你行你上。”

Voice. Voice? You have a voice you remember. You can speak. You open your mouth to utter unimaginable curses, ready to scream at anything and everything for your circumstance-

Nothing comes out.

You try again, ready to curse the living starlight out of these imbeciles-

Still, nothing comes out.

Just then, the opaque barrier fades, leaving only a shimmering lucid membrane. Outside, a sight every human eye has seen: The stars. Not the stars. The night sky. A sea of stars.

An escape.

The only thing familiar between this vessel and Earth: The night sky. A sea of stars.

As the broken and warped sonata plays again, you take a step out into the night sky. A sea of stars.

Behind you, the ship has vanished, leaving all but the same motif hanging in the air. Not air. Space Not space The night sky Yes a sea of stars

You breathe a sigh of relief as the night the true night sings you to sleep in a sea of stars.

71973484 seconds is equal to a bit over 2 years.

Translations from Mandarin (Traditional) to English

- “走。” translated to “Walk.”
- “別讓我再說。走。” translated to “Don’t make me say it again. Walk.”
- “进去。” translated to “Go in.”
- “你行你上。” translated to “You can you up.” Implicitly translated to “If you can do it, do it.”
