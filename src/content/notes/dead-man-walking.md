The depth is indefinite.

The black is invisible.

The void is inconceivable.

And yet brings peace to mind.

Shot through the heart on a field of poppies.

An empty expanse of nonexistence greets those who have met fate.

An eternal silence from which there is no escape.

And yet brings peace to mind.

Innards dissolving at the touch of acid.

To the world's end that everyone is doomed to reach.

And yet brings peace to mind.

Crushed instantly by a toppling building.

And yet brings peace to mind.

Burnt to a crisp by the flames of hell.

Peace.

. . .

“Get your ass up you mule. I need you in the capital by midday.”

*To hell with you.*

. .

“The capsule will open once I leave the room. You better be outside within a minute, Chro.”

*Not this time.*

.

“Debrief for your division starts in an hour.”

*No. Fuck you.*

It’s been through so many times, I know it’s not a lucid dream. It’s not a hallucination. This is reality.

A reality I’ve planned for five years.

Don’t revive anyone you’ve sent to die.

Otherwise it’ll be your end.

. . .

Instead of opening my eyes, as I once had, I imagine casting my hands away from myself. I imagine reaching out to the managram trails, connecting myself to the energy of the crystisis system.

The smooth streams become empty space, interruptions become objects as my spatial awareness expands. Blindsight.

From the streams, a vision connects itself. A figure stands over a glass coffin, three paces away from the starboard end. He holds a clipboard, incessantly tapping a pen against it. The room, 7 meters across, is decorated plainly. A painting. A chair. No windows.

*It will take him 3 seconds to get to the door. It’ll take me half. Half a second.*

A second to get up. Half to block the door. The last spent tearing into his flesh.

As he turns, I snap the connection to the trails, and blindly enter a code onto a keypad inside the capsule. My eyes open slowly as the coffin pressurizes and see the man stumble a bit with the shifting managram paths.

“What in the name of Crys-”

He only managed half a line before his stomach lay on the floor.

*What in the name of Crystisis indeed.*

By the time I started my monologue, I was talking to an empty room.

“Seven years, you’ve rebirthed me twenty-two times. Each time I’ve suffered by the hands of some horrible fate: Torture, Lynching, Execution. Each time I wake up with the memories of pain still within my soul. And each time I’m forced into servitude again at your cruel hands. Now it’s my turn. Watch your windows and clear your closets. A dead man walking has nothing to lose.”
