title: The sun sets on the forest
kind: story
mark: ◠ 友 ◡
---

The sun sets on the forest.

Alone. Cold.

The fire went out yesterday.

No flint since the Friend. No spark since the Friend. No flame since the Friend.

The sun rises.

The sun sets on the forest.

A Stranger arrived.

They saw the fire, the coals, a day burnt out.

He said something and held out a flint.

Don’t trust him yet.

But he gathered the wood. Started the fire.

Warm.

Maybe tomorrow will be better.

The sun rises.

The sun sets on the forest.

The Stranger is back. With blood on their arms and clothes. A knife in their arms.

You pick up a stick. You can defend yourself. Too soon you trusted him.

A thump as something hits the floor. A carcass.

The Stranger says something. From his pack, he grabs a pot. Iron. And a stand.

He gathered the wood. Started the fire.

Water goes in the pot goes on the stand goes on the fire forever.

Smells taunt you but the Stranger holds you back.

He finally lets you go. Rush to the pot.

Burning. You forgot. The smells taunting you.

The Stranger grins, pulls out a bowl and a ladle.

Far too slowly scoops a bowl. Offers it to you.

Nothing has ever tasted so good.

Today was better.

The sun rises.

The sun sets on the forest.

The Stranger is back. They’ve been back many times. Each with an enormous trunk.

He is building something.

Watch him. All day. All night.

Hammer. Nail. Saw. Knife. He has it all in the pack.

The Stranger smiles at you. He knows you’re watching.

Walls go up. A ceiling emerges. Windows appear. A doorframe summoned.

The Stranger says something. He points to the pot.

Soup. The smells have taunted you all day.

He hands you the ladle.

Scoop. Pour…

Hot. Burning.

The Stranger laughs again. Bellowing laughter. Like he’ll never laugh again.

He holds your hands. Guides them.

Scoop. A bowl he hands to you. Pour the soup.

Not as good as yesterday. But still the same soup.

Tomorrow will be even better.

The sun rises.

The sun sets on the forest.

The Stranger is sad.

Tears flow down their face.

The Stranger says something, hiccuping the entire time.

There is no reason to be sad.

There is a soup, a roof, a friend.

Not the Stranger, the Friend.

The pack comes off the Friend.

The pack never comes off the Friend.

Never.

They hold it out to you.

Not your pack. Their pack. You cannot take.

They hold it out to you. The Friend says something.

Not your pack. You cannot take.

The Friend sighs. They go to the house. Put the pack on the door. Take one thing out.

Yellow. With a light.

The Friend asks you something. Gestures something.

You understand. The Friend is leaving.

You don’t want to go.

You are happy.

There is a soup, a roof, the Friend.

The Friend sighs. They raise their hand. Hug you. Hug you forever.

Something loud is above.

Helicopter. You see it.

A ladder drops down.

The Friend steps on the ladder. Raises a last hand to you.

You don’t want to go. There is a soup, a roof, the Friend.

The ladder rises. The Friend is gone.

The sun rises.

The sun sets on the forest.

There is a soup, a roof.

The sun rises.

The sun sets on the forest.

There is a roof.

The sun rises.

The sun sets on the forest.

Alone. Cold.

The fire went out yesterday.

No flint since the Friend. No spark since the Friend. No flame since the Friend.

The sun rises.
