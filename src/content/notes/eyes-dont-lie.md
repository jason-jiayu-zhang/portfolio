title: eyes don’t lie
kind: story

There’s nothing of interest in the deserts of the wastes. Sand flies through the air carelessly, battering itself across the breadth of the canyons bordering the valley. A few pieces hit my eyes. I’m so far removed from the world I don’t even flinch at the pain. A single, lonely tumbleweed runs across the mounds, the last leaf of its deadened branches flying off, instantly shrouded and disintegrated by the storm. It’s like me to an extent. The last hope of fruition lost to the sands of time.

I’ve never been so far out from the Crystalis. So far even the filters of my mask have begun to fail. They’ve never failed. They’re what tether us to the Crystalis. No one knows what happens without them. I doff the mask in a hurry, I won’t be needing it for long. Maybe some of the wastefolk can make a fortune off of it. After all, I spent a fortune myself developing it.

But in the end all they are are tethers controlled by the Crystalis executives. Executives I’ve rebelled against endless times and who hate my ass more than the ticking that keeps them awake at night.

And the masks lie. Everything you see through them is a screen, recorded in time, but recorded nonetheless, and drawn onto your retinas. In reality, you see nothing. And they know this. Certain things seemed not to align when I’d take my mask off: the colors were always dull, everything was more blurred, and certain buildings or objects seemed to disappear.

That drove me to propagate the eradication of masks, or at least alter them. But the people don’t often listen to what’s right. They see instant change and immediate discomfort and scatter to the winds. But their eyes don’t lie. Everything is in their heads.

The mask slides off easily. Each of the mindless peasants is trapped in their own mind that is trapped in the mask. The sand batters my face, occasionally slicing lines of blood into my cheeks. But through the pain, although everpresent, the true colors of the world emerge. Hues that one who has only ever seen dull could not even think to be real.

I should put it back on. I don’t know if I’ve completely disabled the alarms. But temptation is impossible to resist, beckoning me forward.

*This is the true world.* It says in my head.

*This is how you were meant to live.* It echos in my brain.

*Your eyes don’t lie.* Lost in the voices.

I lose track of time and just stare at the colors. Unmoving despite the raging storm.

Lost until a single line of static crosses my ears.

“Target acquired.”

As the colors fade away.

Into darkness.
