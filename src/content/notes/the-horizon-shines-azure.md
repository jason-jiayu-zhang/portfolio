---
title: The Horizon Shines Azure
date: 2023-07-05
kind: story
form: poem
mark: ⁕ 蒼 ⁕
---

Lights on the horizon fill
Sounds move towards my ears, echoing
A single tower standing atop the highest hill
The hill from which glowing lights shine towards
With bells ringing and people singing
Counting seconds
One two three four, forty miles on the first
One two, twenty to the second
One two three four five, fifty very distant
Imagining the cheers from the awestruck
And the lights as bright as the sun
Slowly the sound grows and grows
As the flowers from across the land burst
Until finally cut by two blooms
Flames and vitality
One after another
And utter tranquility
As I draw the helmet back on
Gasping from the smoke of the blooms
Now the horizon glows golden
But when the horizon shines azure
Is when we will have entered the future
