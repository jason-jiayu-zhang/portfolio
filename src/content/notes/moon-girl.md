She ran into the forest the moment of blackest night. Hair trailing through the blistering cold storm, snow and rain and hail and sleet. Even without light, her golden hair seems to glow, as if she had stolen any light the world had to offer. This you saw.

She was so careful, too careful almost. Covering each and every one of her tracks with a brush, making sure the storm would cover them later. Each and every step she methodically took, picking the path that would require her to brush the least amount of snow over her steps. Each and every step growing longer and longer as she gained the confidence that no one had seen her. This you saw.

The day before, her schedule was perfect. No one would have noticed her out of place simply because of how in place she was. Every moment of the day she was exactly where she had been the day before. From sunrise to sunset she walked the exact same path on the exact same timing as she had every 5th for the past four months. This you saw.

Nothing could stop her plan but you.

☾ 月 ☽

You’ve been watching her. Every day since the night of the Court Assassination, the day the king was murdered. They found no witness, only a single piece of paper with a conversation scrawled on it in charcoal.

> I need to warn the king.
> Usually, people with good will toward the crown enter the grand hall through the front doors. Most others are not so friendly. Tell me what you need.
> How do I know I can trust you?
> You can’t. But if you tell me nothing, I have all the reason to send you to the guards.
> You’re not that type.
> Correct, sun girl. Usually, you’d be dead already. But the early death of children is something I’d prefer to avoid.
> You have to let the king know that a trap has been set in the hall. I’m not sure what exactly will happen, but, if nothing happens, he will die tonight.
> Aren’t you a bit too young for a job like this?
> That’s beside the point! You need to let me warn someon

The only clue was a line of golden hair, caught on the window of the palace. A line of golden thread as golden as the sun. Something fitting of a sun girl. Yet no one at the palace that night had seen anyone of the sort.

Months after the incident, a trail finally emerged. Another thread, the same make, material, and design, caught on the edge of a bush to a closed neighborhood. Within that bush, a child sized space had been carved out with a sewing kit with golden and silver threads stashed within it. And sewn onto the lacing of the kit was one name: “moon girl”, and a place with removed stitching, which after asking many seamstresses was believed to read “sun girl”.

☾ 月 ☽

Tediously, the girl continues her trek through the snow-covered prairie until finally reaching the forest line. She looks around nervously, left, right, and center. Almost for a second, it feels like she stares into your soul through the darkened window. But she doesn’t notice. Continuing a few steps into the forest, she pauses, seeming to utter an incantation, as the forest grows immeasurably more dense in front of your eyes. Between the canines of the newly grown trees, a golden light sneaks through, rays of it growing brighter and brighter, until disappearing altogether. Or seem to disappear. On a second glance, they haven’t just vanished, but instead transformed into a white and silver light, blending into the snow around it.

In an instant, the wall of trees drops, and from within, an entirely new figure emerges, the dull gold clothing she’d previously been wearing replaced with a silver gown, trailing in the snow behind her. Even as the hail rains down, she doesn’t seem to flinch at all, each bead of sleet simply ricocheting off of her. Other than the clear growth in height, her hair is the most striking difference. The previously almost dirty blonde hair now shines silver, radiating light as if reflecting every source perfectly.

As if hearing the malice in your mind towards her, the moon girl snaps her head to face you, still across the field. And within your mind, you hear a soft voice:

“I saw you earlier, you know. It’s not good to spy on people.”

You jump in fright, taking your eyes off the window for just a moment as you blink. And within that moment, the moon girl disappears.

Only then does it hit you.

A new body for the sun girl, moon. A new event for the night, coronation. A new omen for the nation, death.

Someone is going to die tonight.

No, the Queen is going to die tonight.
